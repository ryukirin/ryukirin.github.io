# First Attempt at Hardcoding Subtitles

## Software

Subtitle editing: Aegisub

Subtitle hardcoding: MeGUI 2896 + AviSynth 2.6.0

MeGUI works right after extracting the archive. AviSynth needs to be installed, and on the component selection step of the installer I selected everything (that may not matter).

With a 29xx version of MeGUI, adding a subtitle file is likely to fail with `Plugin was designed for a later version of Avisynth(8)`, so it is best to turn off automatic updates.

## Pitfalls

I started with Xiaowan Toolbox (小丸工具箱), but it kept failing to recognize the font styles in my subtitle file, and the output came out broken (another, shorter video with simpler styles was fine).

Worth trying: [【Aegisub//小丸工具箱】添加字幕步骤（渔休整理）](https://www.bilibili.com/read/cv1629065)

Next I tried [StarTools](https://github.com/hoshinohikari/StarTools/blob/master/README_CN.md). It is said to be very good, but it did not suit me (I could not figure out how to use it).

After that I tried [NegativeEncoder (消极压制)](https://github.com/hoshinohikari/NegativeEncoder) (it turned out to be from the same developer as StarTools).

In the end I settled on MeGUI.

That's all.
